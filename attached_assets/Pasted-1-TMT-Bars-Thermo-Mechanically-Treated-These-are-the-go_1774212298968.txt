1. TMT Bars (Thermo-Mechanically Treated)
These are the gold standard for modern construction. They undergo a specialized heat treatment process (quenching and tempering) that creates a hard outer core and a soft inner core.  
• Specifications: Commonly available in grades Fe415, Fe500, Fe550, and Fe600. (The number represents the yield strength in N/mm^2).  
• Best For: All residential and commercial buildings, bridges, and high-rise structures.  
• Key Feature: Excellent ductility, high weldability, and superior earthquake resistance.  
2. TMX Bars (Thermex)
TMX is an advanced version of TMT bars, often referred to as "new generation" bars. They are processed using German Thermex technology.  
• Specifications: Similar to TMT but with a more refined grain structure and higher heat resistance.
• Best For: Critical infrastructure in fire-prone zones or areas with extreme temperatures.  
• Key Feature: Higher thermal stability (maintains strength even at 600°C).
3. HSD Bars (High Strength Deformed)
These bars are cold-twisted, which increases their strength but can introduce internal stresses.  
• Specifications: Usually found in grades like Fe415.  
• Best For: General reinforcement in smaller structures.
• Key Feature: The ribs (deformations) on the surface provide a better bond with concrete compared to smooth bars.  
4. Corrosion Resistant Steel (CRS)
Also known as Epoxy-Coated or Galvanized Rebar, these are treated to withstand harsh environments.
• Specifications: Often TMT bars treated with a fusion-bonded epoxy coating or chrome/copper additives in the alloy.  
• Best For: Coastal areas, marine structures, and bridges over saltwater.
• Key Feature: Prevents rust from expanding inside the concrete, which is the leading cause of structural "spalling."
5. Mild Steel Plain Bars
Unlike the others, these have a smooth surface without ribs or lugs.
• Specifications: Grade Fe250.
• Best For: Small-scale projects, dowels in expansion joints, or stirrups (though TMT is now preferred for stirrups too).
• Key Feature: Highly ductile and easy to cut/bend, but has a weaker bond with concrete.
